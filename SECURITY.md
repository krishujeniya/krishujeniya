# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or privacy issue in this portfolio, repository, or related services, please report it responsibly.

### Contact
- **Email:** [ukideashare0021@gmail.com](mailto:ukideashare0021@gmail.com)
- **Subject line:** `[Security Vulnerability Report] <Brief Description>`

### What to include
- A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Step-by-step reproduction instructions or a minimal proof of concept (PoC).
- Affected files, URLs, endpoints, or components.

### Policy & Timeline
- You will receive an initial response acknowledging receipt within 48 hours.
- A remediation or mitigation plan will be communicated within 7 business days.
- Please do not publicly disclose the issue until it has been reviewed and addressed.
- Good-faith vulnerability reports conducted in accordance with this policy will receive full credit and no legal action.
